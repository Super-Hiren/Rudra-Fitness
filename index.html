On the floor

Three zones, all on the same membership. Nothing here is roped off for a premium tier.

Machines

Plate-loaded press, leg press, rows and racks set along the window line. Enough stations that you are not waiting behind someone through your whole session.

Free weights

Dumbbells from light pairs up to the heavy end, flat and incline benches, cables, and a bag in the corner for the days you want to hit something.

Cardio deck

Treadmills facing the glass, plus cycles for steady sessions, warm-ups and the group cycling class.

Training and classes

Come in and lift on your own, or take a slot with a trainer. Classes run in the studio and online.

  • Weight training

    Structured lifting with someone on the floor to check your form and load.

  • Personal training

    One-to-one sessions built around what you are actually trying to change.

  • Nutrition consulting

    A diet plan that fits Surat food, your budget and your working hours.

  • Cycling

    Group cycling for hard cardio without the joint impact of running.

  • Zumba

    Dance-led cardio in a group. No experience needed, just turn up.

  • Aerobics

    Full-body conditioning, an easy way in if the weights floor feels intimidating.

  • Online classes

    Join the session from home on the days you cannot get to the gym.

When to come

The floor fills up after work like every gym in Surat. If your schedule is flexible, the early afternoon is the easiest time to get a full session in.
